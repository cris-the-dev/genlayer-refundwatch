Harbour Lights Concert

Saturday evening · Main Hall, 14 Quay Road

Brass band classics and sea shanties on the harbour stage, with fireworks over the water at the close.

Doors open 30 minutes before the start. Seating is unreserved. Bar and cloakroom open all evening.

Questions: box office, 9:00-17:00 on weekdays.
